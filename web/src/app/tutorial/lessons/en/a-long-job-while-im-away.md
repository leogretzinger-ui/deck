# A long job while I'm away from my desk

## Sessions run on the server
advance_on: continue

Sessions run on the Deck machine, not in your browser. You can close the tab and the agent keeps working. If Deck runs on another machine, you can close your laptop too.

## Open the same session on your phone
advance_on: continue
primary: Show the address
action: show-address

Open Deck's address on your phone and sign in. Pick a session in the side panel to see the same terminal. Use the key row above the keyboard for Escape, Tab, Control and the arrows.

## Take control, or just watch
advance_on: continue

Only one browser can type at a time. If your laptop still has the session, choose Take control on your phone to type there.
