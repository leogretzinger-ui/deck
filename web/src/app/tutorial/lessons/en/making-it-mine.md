# Making it mine

## Terminal and theme
advance_on: continue

Open the global menu to change the terminal font size and color scheme. Choose Auto-color projects to give each project its own tint in the side panel.

## Keyboard
advance_on: continue

Press Tab to move through buttons and menus. Menu items show their shortcuts next to them.
